A note from the partners i.

An unhurried approach to what is rarely an easy time.

When someone dies, their family is often left with a house, a car and a lifetime of possessions to deal with, usually when they would rather deal with nothing at all. Our work is to make that part simpler.

We buy directly, so there is no auction to wait for, no commission to pay and no stream of strangers walking through the house. We assess each asset carefully, show you the market evidence behind our valuation and put our offer in writing.

Many of the families we work with are introduced by their solicitor. Others find us themselves. Either way, the first conversation is confidential and commits you to nothing.

If our offer is not right for you, we will say so plainly and, where we can, point you towards a better route. We would rather be trusted than busy.

What we acquireii.

Four categories, held to one standard of care.

  1. I.

    Property & Land

    Houses, flats and land in any condition, whether vacant, tenanted, in need of work or still full of contents. We buy as seen, so you need not clear or repair anything.

    TypicallyFamily homes · Flats & apartments · Land & outbuildings · Properties needing renovation
  2. II.

    Motor Vehicles

    Cars, motorcycles, vans and caravans, including vehicles that have been off the road or in storage for many years. We arrange enclosed collection and deal with the paperwork.

    TypicallyEveryday cars · Prestige & classic cars · Motorcycles · Caravans & motorhomes
  3. III.

    Gold & Valuables

    Precious metals and jewellery, independently assayed and priced against the live market rate on the day. Each piece is itemised in writing.

    TypicallyBullion & sovereigns · Fine jewellery · Watches · Silver & silverware
  4. IV.

    Fine Possessions

    Antiques, art, furniture and collections, catalogued on site and removed with care. We can also take entire household contents.

    TypicallyAntique furniture · Paintings & prints · Collections · Full house contents
How we workiii.

A clear process, at a pace you set.

First

A conversation

A private discussion, by telephone or in person, about the estate, the assets and what matters most to you.

Second

An assessment

A discreet visit at a time of your choosing. Where an asset calls for it, a specialist inspects it.

Third

A written offer

A clear offer that sets out the evidence behind each valuation. It carries no obligation and no deadline.

Finally

Completion

Secure payment and careful collection, coordinated with your solicitor or executor.

Working alongside your solicitor

We are used to working with solicitors and executors and are happy to take instruction through them. An offer can be agreed before the grant of probate, with completion following once the grant is issued.

Our principlesiv.

Four commitments behind every acquisition we make.

1

Discretion

Every enquiry is confidential. We arrive in unmarked vehicles and never publicise a purchase.

2

Fair value

Our offers rest on current market evidence, and we show you that evidence so you can judge for yourself.

3

Your timing

We can complete within days when estate costs are pressing, or wait patiently while probate takes its course.

4

Plain dealing

We charge no fees and attach no conditions, and our offers come in writing. If we cannot help, we will tell you so.

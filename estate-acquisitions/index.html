ii.The process

Unhurried,and entirely clear.

You decide the pace and there is no obligation at any stage. Where you have appointed a solicitor or executor, we work alongside them.

01

Conversation

A private, unhurried discussion about the estate, the assets and what matters most to you.

By telephone or in person
02

Assessment

A discreet visit at a time of your choosing. Specialists inspect where an asset calls for it.

Usually within days
03

Written offer

A clear offer in writing that sets out the market evidence behind each valuation.

Without obligation
04

Completion

Secure payment, careful collection and coordination with your solicitor or executor.

On your timeline
iii.Our principles

What you canrely upon.

We would rather earn your trust than ask for it. These four commitments guide every acquisition we make.

i.

Discretion

We keep every enquiry in confidence, use unmarked vehicles and never publicise a purchase.

ii.

Fair value

Our offers rest on current market evidence, and we show you that evidence openly.

iii.

Timing

We can move within days to meet estate costs, or wait patiently while probate takes its course.

iv.

Experience

We work closely with executors, solicitors and families, and we know the probate process in depth.
